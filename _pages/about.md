Hello! I am an M.S. student at the Korea Advanced
Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST),
Daejeon, South Korea.

I received my B.Eng. degree in Automation from
Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, China,
in 2026.

My research interests lie in aerial robotics,
nonlinear control, and cooperative aerial
transportation. I am particularly interested in
the modeling, control, and experimental validation
of UAV systems carrying suspended payloads.
